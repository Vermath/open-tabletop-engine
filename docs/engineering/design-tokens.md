# Web design tokens

The Midnight and Ember palettes live in the same stylesheet. Use the surface scale from the canvas background through raised cards, the two line strengths for divisions and emphasis, and the text and muted text roles for foregrounds. Use success, warning, danger and info for status. Accent belongs to selected, focused and actionable controls.

The type scale has seven steps. Use 13 px for body text and at least 12 px for regular labels and secondary information. Reserve 11 px for uppercase labels. Use the 4 px spacing scale and choose the smallest radius and elevation that communicate the control or panel hierarchy.

Fast motion is for feedback, normal motion is for transitions between interface states, and slow motion is for large changes. Use the enter and exit curves with their respective directions. Respect the user's reduced-motion preference and the OS setting.

The budget test measures literal colour occurrences, distinct untokenized font-size values, and distinct animation and transition durations. When a section adopts tokens, lower its measured budgets alongside the code.
