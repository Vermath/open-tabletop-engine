## Findings and acceptance criteria

| ID | Finding | Required behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| G01 | Removing a campaign member changed token ownership in unrelated campaigns. | Only tokens in the affected campaign change; other ownership and revisions remain intact. |
| G02 | Removing an organization member could orphan campaigns they own. | Reject removal atomically until campaign ownership is transferred, without exposing inaccessible campaign details. |
| G03 | Session, world, and handout editors paired stale content with newer revision tokens. | Dirty/recovered drafts retain their baseline; clean views refresh; conflicting content can be reviewed and explicitly reloaded. |
| G04 | Session start could activate a removed or unsaved scene link. | Activation uses persisted valid links and pending edits must be saved first. |
| G05 | Adding a higher-initiative combatant rejected an otherwise valid roster edit. | Preserve the active combatant's identity without executing turn effects. |
| G06 | Editing initiative silently changed whose turn it was. | Reordering preserves the active combatant. |
| G07 | Previous at the first combat turn could wrap forward and execute effects. | The first reachable turn is a lower boundary. |
| G08 | Reindexing initiative made spent actions available again. | Roster/order changes preserve effective action and mastery usage; genuine new turns reset turn resources. |
| G09 | Pasted tokens could land partly or wholly outside the destination map. | Keep copies reachable within destination bounds and preserve formation where possible. |
| G10 | Moving an annotation against a map edge changed its shape. | Bound one shared translation and preserve geometry through API normalization and optimistic reconciliation. |
| G11 | Clicking precisely placed tokens snapped their position. | Selection alone creates neither a move nor an undo entry; real dragging still works. |
| G12 | Live inspector labels and icons were clipped on desktop and phones. | Full labels fit their controls and remain keyboard reachable. |
| G13 | Phone workspace navigation clipped Live Table, AI Studio, and Manage. | Fit icons and labels within the existing bottom rail and touch targets. |
| G14 | Long connection errors pushed the mobile retry UI off-screen. | Wrap long diagnostics and keep recovery controls within the viewport. |
